I am a fifth-year Computer Science Ph.D. candidate in the Future Reality Lab at NYU, advised by Prof. Ken Perlin. I study how interactive systems can help people think, create, and work together.

With AI, I build proactive multimodal agents that understand people in context: inferring an individual's latent, evolving intent, and supporting groups through creativity support and by connecting fragmented knowledge across members.

In mixed reality, I build collaborative XR environments, generative 3D authoring tools, and robotic and shape-changing interfaces for physical interaction.

My work has been published and demoed at UIST, DIS, VRST (Best Paper Award), SIGGRAPH, TEI, and ICRA. I have also been a research intern at Microsoft Research in the Deep Learning Group, working with Andrzej Banburski-Fahey and Nebojsa Jojic, and at the MIT Media Lab in Hiroshi Ishii's Tangible Media Group.

SEAM: Situated Executable Associative Memory for a Co-located Community

A shared memory for a community that connects, reveals, and acts

Keru Wang, Michael Xu, Ken Perlin, Nebojsa Jojic, Andrzej Banburski-Fahey

Under review — preprint available upon request

Abstract

In a group, what each person knows sits apart from everyone else's, and whether those pieces ever meet depends on who happens to talk to whom. Taking inspiration from how a single mind works with memory, we built a shared memory that does the same for a community, prototyped as a social app. Members post everyday moments; LLM agents work out how fragments relate, distill knowledge about the group, and reply in a situated and associative way, while instructions embedded in posts shape how the system behaves. We deployed SEAM to 46 people in a co-located community with no task assigned and ran a follow-up workshop with 14. Members shared their days, questioned the memory about their community, and instructed it to track routines and run games, and its replies introduced them to people and subjects they had never encountered.

An Investigation of Proactive AI Agents for Reducing Idea Stagnation in Group Brainstorming

A proactive agent that steps in when group ideation stalls

Keru Wang, Pincun Liu, Saining Xie, Ken Perlin

Under review — preprint available upon request

Abstract

We designed a proactive AI agent that monitors group brainstorming and intervenes at opportune moments to counter idea stagnation. Through two user studies with 18 participants, we found that the proactive agent boosted idea generation, depth, and novelty compared to a reactive baseline, though users still preferred reactive support for its greater sense of control.

Should I Speak Up? Aligning Proactive AI Interventions with Human Expectations in Group Brainstorming

When should an AI speak up in a group discussion?

Keru Wang, Pincun Liu, Saining Xie, Ken Perlin

Under review — preprint available upon request

Abstract

We explore how proactive AI agents can step in at the right moments to keep group brainstorming productive and natural. Using a custom LLM system that monitors discussions and explains its reasoning, we compared its timing judgement to expert evaluations. The study uncovers what makes AI interventions feel helpful versus disruptive, guiding the design of more intuitive and collaborative AI partners.

Flying Together: Human-Guided Immersive Shared Control for Aerial Robot Teams in Unknown Environments

VR shared control for guiding teams of drones

Lou De Bel-Air, Luca Morando, Ruitao Chen, Keru Wang, Benjamin Jarvis, Charbel Toumieh, Yang Zhou, Ken Perlin, Dario Floreano, Giuseppe Loianno

IEEE ICRA 2026

arXiv | PDF | Video

Abstract

We developed a VR-based shared control framework that allows operators to guide teams of drones in complex, unknown environments. A motion-primitive planner integrates real-time user input to generate collision-free trajectories, while an admittance controller enables flexible human influence. Experiments show that this immersive, human-in-the-loop approach enhances obstacle avoidance, coordination, and operator efficiency.

Celestial Blossom: A Collaborative, Meditative VR Journey of Fractal Art and Music

Co-creating fractal art and music together in VR

Pincun Liu, Keru Wang, Yi Wu, Xiaoan Liu, Yushen Hu, Zhu Wang, Agnieszka Roginska, Ken Perlin

ACM SIGGRAPH Asia XR 2025

ACM | PDF | Video

Abstract

Celestial Blossom is a collaborative, meditative VR experience where participants co-create generative fractal art through hand-drawn sketches, transforming movements into evolving audiovisual symphonies. Set in a celestial void, strokes morph into glowing fractal particles, from microscopic grains to cosmic stars, each shaping sonic textures and evolving into an immersive soundscape. Multiplayer interaction allows users to weave their creations into a shared immersive composition. At the end, participants receive a printed rendering of their artwork, which can be scanned to revisit the fractals and melodies they created in VR.

Audio-influenced Pseudo-haptics: A Review of Effects, Applications, and Research Directions

How sound can create a sense of touch

Keru Wang, Yi Wu, Pincun Liu, Zhu Wang, Agnieszka Roginska, Qi Sun, Ken Perlin

AM.ICAD 2025

Paper | PDF

Abstract

This survey explores how auditory cues can evoke pseudo-haptic sensations, offering a cost-effective alternative to traditional haptic devices. We review existing research on audio-influenced pseudo-haptics, summarize mappings between sound and touch perception, and outline future opportunities for creating more immersive and accessible interactive systems.

Generative Terrain Authoring with Mid-air Hand Sketching in Virtual Reality

Sketching landscapes in mid-air with a generative model

ACM VRST 2024  🏆 Best Paper Award

ACM | PDF | Video

Abstract

We present a VR-based terrain generation system that uses hand gestures and a generative model for fast landscape prototyping. Users draw and modify mid-air strokes to outline shapes, and a Conditional GAN, trained on real terrains' height maps, generates realistic landscapes blending user input with training features.
