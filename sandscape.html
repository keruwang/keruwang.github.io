SandScape

Installation · on permanent view at the MIT Museum

A table of sand for shaping landscapes by hand. As the sand moves, the table reads its new shape and projects an analysis of the terrain back onto it: its height, its contour lines, where rain would run off it. A screen beside it shows the same landscape in 3D.

I was the lead artist of the MIT Museum’s SandScape (2022), made in collaboration with the MIT Tangible Media Group. It is on permanent view in the museum’s Radical Atoms exhibition.

The first SandScape was made in 2003 by Hiroshi Ishii’s Tangible Media Group at the MIT Media Lab, with the Senseable City Lab.
